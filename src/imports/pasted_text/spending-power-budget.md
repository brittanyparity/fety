# Figma Make Prompt — Spending Power Budget

Design a polished, premium, modern spreadsheet template called **“Spending Power Budget.”**

This is an Etsy digital product designed to help users manage their money using a **cash-flow / zero-balance budgeting approach**.

The core concept is:

**“Know exactly how much spending power you have today.”**

Instead of focusing only on monthly income and expenses, this budget should visually show how money flows through the user's accounts over time, including their **beginning balance, money coming in, money going out, available spending power, and ending balance for every day.**

The design should feel like a premium personal-finance dashboard rather than a traditional boring spreadsheet.

---

## Overall Design Direction

Create a sophisticated, clean, highly organized financial spreadsheet interface.

Visual style:

* Modern
* Minimal
* Premium
* Financial-tech inspired
* Extremely easy to understand
* Friendly rather than corporate
* Designed for everyday consumers
* Appropriate for an Etsy digital download

Use a restrained color palette with:

* Warm white / off-white background
* Dark charcoal text
* Soft neutral gray
* One primary accent color for positive financial information
* One secondary accent color for warnings/attention
* Subtle green for positive cash flow
* Subtle red/coral for overspending or negative cash flow

Avoid excessive decoration.

Use:

* Rounded cards
* Clean tables
* Clear section headers
* Subtle borders
* Small icons where appropriate
* Strong typography hierarchy
* Plenty of whitespace

The spreadsheet should feel similar to a polished modern financial app such as a personal finance dashboard, but it must still clearly look like a **spreadsheet template**.

# DESIGN THE SPREADSHEET, NOT THE FORMULAS

IMPORTANT:

Do NOT attempt to build the underlying spreadsheet formulas, calculations, scripts, automations, or data logic.

Do NOT create a functioning Google Sheets/Excel backend.

Focus exclusively on creating the **visual design, layout, information architecture, tables, dashboard components, charts, cards, filters, and user experience.**

Use realistic sample financial data only as placeholder content so the interface looks complete.

The final design should be detailed enough that the spreadsheet can later be recreated in **Google Sheets or Excel** using formulas, dropdowns, conditional formatting, pivot tables, and charts.

Create a cohesive design system across the entire workbook, including consistent typography, spacing, cards, tables, filters, charts, icons, and status indicators.
